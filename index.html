Smart Assist, only when you want it

Smart Assist can help you understand blocked tiles and better moves. It can be turned ON or OFF anytime from the gameplay bottom bar or Settings.

  • ✓Explains why a tile may be blocked.
  • ✓Helps players learn Mahjong-style logic without constant interruption.
  • ✓Optional by design, so experienced players can keep the board clean.

Summary: Mahjong Solitaire Match is an Android puzzle game. The app stores gameplay progress, settings, saved sessions, and optional notification-reminder preferences locally on your device. The app may use Google services such as Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), and Google Play Billing for analytics, crash diagnostics, ads, consent handling, and Premium purchase handling. The app does not require an account for core gameplay.

1) Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Mahjong Solitaire Match (“App”, “we”, “us”, “our”) accesses, processes, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

2) Information we access or process

2.1 Information stored locally on your device

  • Game settings: The App may store local settings such as sound effects, background music, vibration, theme mode, tutorial-tip state, Smart Assist state, notification reminder preference, and similar comfort or interface preferences.
  • Game progress: The App may store local gameplay data such as unlocked levels, completed levels, stars, score, lives, hints, shuffles, Premium cached state, and premium daily bonus state.
  • Saved sessions: The App may store a local active gameplay session so you can resume a game later. This may include selected difficulty, selected mode, level number, hint/shuffle counts, retry counts, tile states, and related in-progress gameplay state.
  • Notification reminders: If you enable Daily Challenge reminders, the App may store your reminder preference locally and may schedule local device notifications. These reminders are optional and can be disabled in the App or through Android settings.

2.2 Information processed by third-party services

The App may use third-party Google services. These services may process data according to their own policies, SDK behavior, and your device, region, consent choices, and Google account settings.

  • Firebase Analytics: app usage analytics, gameplay event signals, screen interactions, performance signals, and similar analytics data.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports, diagnostics, device/app technical signals, and error data used to improve app stability and troubleshoot problems.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): ad requests, ad impressions, interactions, device/app identifiers, IP address, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals in the free version where ads are shown.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): consent status and privacy-choice handling related to ads and privacy choices, where required.
  • Google Play Billing: purchase state, entitlement validation, purchase tokens, restore-purchase signals, and related billing data necessary to unlock and restore Premium through Google Play.

2.3 Information we do not intentionally request for core gameplay

The App is not designed around account creation, profile hosting, contact upload, precise location collection, camera access, microphone access, or private file browsing for core gameplay.

3) How we use information

  • To provide puzzle gameplay, progress tracking, level unlocking, daily challenges, and session resume.
  • To store your settings, preferences, notification-reminder choices, and gameplay state locally on your device.
  • To show ads in the free version, where applicable.
  • To process Premium purchase and restore flows through Google Play Billing.
  • To improve app stability, performance, balancing, and debugging through diagnostics and analytics.
  • To help detect crashes, abuse, fraud, and technical misuse.
  • To manage consent and privacy options for ads, where required.

4) Notifications and reminders

The App may offer optional local notifications such as Daily Challenge reminders. Notifications are not required to play the game. On supported Android versions, the App will request your permission before sending notifications. You can turn reminders off in the App settings or disable notifications through Android system settings.

5) Advertising and Premium

  • Free version: The free version of the App may display ads, including rewarded ads and interstitial ads where applicable.
  • Premium: Premium is a one-time unlock processed through Google Play Billing. Premium may remove ads and unlock Premium benefits depending on the current version of the App.
  • Reward-based bonuses: The App may offer optional in-game bonuses, such as hints or shuffles, through rewarded ad flows. Related reward state may be stored locally on your device.

6) Permissions and access

The App requests only the access needed for the features it currently offers.

  • VIBRATE - to provide optional haptic feedback during gameplay where supported by the device and your settings.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS - to send optional Daily Challenge reminder notifications on Android versions where notification permission is required. This feature is optional and can be disabled.
  • Network access through third-party SDKs and platform services - to support ads, consent handling, analytics, crash reporting, and Google Play Billing where applicable.

No in-app account required for core use.
No direct payment card handling by us.
We do not sell your personal data.
